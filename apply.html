Why we ask for this information

This is a demonstration form for a student project. Please do not enter real personal details; use made-up test data. Your answers are sent to the Swinburne test script, not to Apple, and are not kept as a job application.

In a real application we would only ask for details needed to assess you and contact you: your date of birth confirms your identity and that you are old enough to work, and gender is used only for equal-opportunity reporting, which is why "Prefer not to say" is always accepted. Information about adjustments you need is used only to arrange your interview.
