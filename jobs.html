Front-End Developer

Reference HD417

About the team

  • Six people: three developers, one designer, one content specialist, one lead.
  • Two days a week in the office, three remote.
  • We review each other's code. Nothing ships without a second pair of eyes.
  • Every release must pass the same accessibility checks we ask of applicants.

About the role

You will build and maintain the customer-facing pages of our booking and enquiry platforms. Most of your week is hand-written HTML, CSS and templating work, with an emphasis on pages that stay fast and usable on older devices and slow connections.

Salary
AUD 78,000 - 92,000 per year, full time, plus superannuation
Reporting line
Reports to the Web Team Lead, who reports to the Head of Digital Delivery
Contract
Ongoing, full time (38 hours per week)
Closing date
Friday 5:00pm, four weeks after this advertisement appears

Key responsibilities

  • Build responsive page templates from designs, using semantic HTML and CSS.
  • Keep the shared stylesheet organised, documented and free of dead rules.
  • Test each change against keyboard navigation and a screen reader before release.
  • Fix defects raised by the support team, starting with those that block a task.
  • Review a teammate's work at least twice a week and explain your reasoning.

Essential requirements

  • Demonstrated ability to write valid, semantic HTML5 without a framework.
  • Confident with CSS layout, including Flexbox and Grid, and with responsive design.
  • Working knowledge of WCAG 2.1 Level AA and how to test against it.
  • Able to explain a technical decision to a non-technical colleague.
  • Comfortable using version control day to day.

Preferable requirements

  • Experience supporting a site that is already in production.
  • Familiarity with a templating language or static site generator.
  • Exposure to automated accessibility or performance testing tools.
  • Interest in mentoring someone earlier in their career.

Accessibility and Content Specialist

Reference HD892

Good to know

  • This is a new role, so you will help shape what it becomes.
  • You do not need to write production code, but you will read it.
  • Part-time arrangements are genuinely available for this position.

About the role

You will make sure what we publish can actually be used and understood. That covers auditing pages against accessibility standards, rewriting copy that confuses people, and working with developers early enough that problems are designed out rather than patched later.

Salary
AUD 72,000 - 84,000 per year, full time, plus superannuation (pro rata if part time)
Reporting line
Reports to the Head of Digital Delivery, working alongside the Web Team Lead
Contract
Ongoing; full time or part time from 0.6 FTE
Closing date
Friday 5:00pm, four weeks after this advertisement appears

Key responsibilities

  • Audit published pages against WCAG 2.1 Level AA and record what you find.
  • Rewrite page copy, form labels and error messages in plain English.
  • Run short usability sessions and turn what you observe into specific changes.
  • Advise designers and developers while a feature is still being planned.
  • Maintain the internal content style guide.

Essential requirements

  • Practical experience applying accessibility standards to real web pages.
  • Strong written English and the judgement to cut words that add nothing.
  • Able to read HTML and CSS well enough to describe a fix precisely.
  • Evidence of working directly with the people who use a service.

Preferable requirements

  • Experience with screen readers such as NVDA, JAWS or VoiceOver.
  • Background in technical writing, editing or education.
  • Familiarity with a content management system.

How to apply

Follow these steps in order. Applications missing a reference number cannot be matched to a role.

  1. Note the five character reference number of the role you want, for example HD417.
  2. Open the application form.
  3. Type that reference number into the Job reference number field yourself. The link above does not fill it in for you.
  4. Complete every remaining field, then check your email address and phone number before submitting.
  5. Submit the form once. You will not receive an automatic confirmation email from this prototype.

Applying for both roles? Send a separate application for each reference number. One form submission covers one vacancy.
