Build the services people depend on

How hiring works here

Every applicant goes through the same five stages. No stage is a surprise.

Harbourline Digital hiring process, stage by stage
Phase Stage What to expect
What happens Typical timing
Apply 1. Online application You send the application form with the reference number of the role. Any time before the closing date
2. Shortlisting Two team members read every application against the essential requirements. Within 5 working days of closing
Assess 3. Skills conversation A 45 minute call about your work. You may bring examples instead of doing a test. Week 1 after shortlisting
4. Team interview You meet the people you would work with and ask them anything. Week 2 after shortlisting
Decide 5. Offer and onboarding We call with a decision either way, and give feedback if you ask for it. Within 5 working days of the interview

Working towards a workplace that includes First Peoples

Harbourline Digital encourages applications from Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples for every role advertised on this site. We recognise Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples as the Traditional Owners of the lands on which we work, and we recognise their continuing connection to land, waters, knowledge and community.

Why this sits on a careers page

A recruitment site is where an employer's commitments are tested. Saying that we welcome First Nations applicants means little unless the hiring process reflects it, so we have made three specific changes to the process described above:

  • Applicants may bring existing work or community experience to the skills conversation instead of completing a separate technical test.
  • Every applicant can ask for feedback at any stage, not only after a final decision.
  • Our essential requirements are kept deliberately short, so people who took a non-traditional route into web work are not screened out before anyone reads their application.
