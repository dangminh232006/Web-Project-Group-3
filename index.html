Who we are

The Apple Online Store web team builds the pages where customers explore products, compare models, check delivery and pickup options, and apply for trade-in and financing. Designers, front-end developers and accessibility specialists work together in small teams, and every change is tested with real users before it goes live.

What we value

  • Accessibility: our products are designed for everyone, and so are our web pages.
  • Privacy: we only ask for the information we really need.
  • Simplicity: clear words and clean layouts beat clever tricks.
  • Craft: we validate our code and sweat the small details.

How hiring works

Every applicant goes through the same steps, so nothing comes as a surprise.

Apple Online Store web team: hiring process
Stage Step What to expect
What happens Typical timing
Apply 1. Online application You complete the application form using the reference number of the role. Any time before the closing date
2. Application review Two members of the team read your application against the essential requirements. You can ask for feedback at this and every later step. Within 5 working days
Interview 3. Phone conversation A 30 minute call with a recruiter about your experience and the role. Week 1 after review
4. Team interview You talk through work you are proud of (from a job, study or community project) and meet the people you would work with. You can bring a support person. Week 2 after review
Offer 5. Decision We call you with our decision either way and give feedback if you ask for it. Within 5 working days of the interview
Total time from application to decision About 4 weeks

Our commitment to inclusive hiring

We warmly encourage applications from Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples for every role on this site. A careers page is where an employer's words are put to the test, so we have built three commitments into the hiring process described above:

  1. You can share work, study or community experience instead of a formal portfolio.
  2. You can ask for a support person or a culturally safe contact at any stage of the process.
  3. You can ask for feedback at every step, not only after a final decision.

Why this is here: websites like this one are built on land that has been cared for by First Nations peoples for more than 60,000 years. Recognising that, and making the hiring process genuinely open, is part of building a fair and inclusive workplace.
